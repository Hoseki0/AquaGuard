Firmware Pin Mappings

  • Water Depth ADC: GPIO 36 (VP)
  • Solenoid Relay (Inflow): GPIO 26 (Active LOW)
  • Drain Pump Relay: GPIO 27 (Active LOW)
  • Auxiliary Relays: GPIO 14, GPIO 12
  • Built-in Status LED: GPIO 2

⚡ Inductive Kickback Protection

Both the 12V Solenoid Valve and Diaphragm Pump are inductive coils. You MUST install flyback diodes (1N4007 / 1N5408) with the striped cathode band pointing to (+12V) to protect the ESP32 from fatal +100V voltage spikes.
